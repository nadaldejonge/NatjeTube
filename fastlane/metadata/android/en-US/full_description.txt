NatjeTube is an independent Android TV client based on SmartTube. Version 2.0 adds a refreshed dark interface and NatjeNightMode: warmth, dimming, presets, custom profiles and offline scheduling. Existing playback, account linking, search, subtitles and SponsorBlock use the existing architecture. Night warmth is a tint, not calibrated display control. Physical TV/HDR testing is still pending. Original licences and attribution are retained. Download signed releases from https://github.com/nadaldejonge/NatjeTube/releases
